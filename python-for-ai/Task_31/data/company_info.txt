NovaTech Solutions is a fictional technology company founded in 2020.

The company is headquartered in Pune, India, and specializes in
artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and software development.

NovaTech Solutions develops intelligent software solutions for
businesses and educational institutions.

The company's mission is to make advanced technology accessible
and useful for organizations of all sizes.

The company has approximately 150 employees and operates with
teams in software engineering, artificial intelligence, research,
marketing, and customer support.
