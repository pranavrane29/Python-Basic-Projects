COLLEGE RULES AND REGULATIONS

Attendance:
Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75 percent
in each subject. Students with attendance below the required
percentage may not be permitted to appear for the semester
examination unless they receive an approved condonation.

Library:
Students may borrow up to three books at a time.
Books must be returned within 14 days. A late return may
result in a fine according to college regulations.

Examinations:
Students must carry their valid college identity card
during examinations. Students must arrive at least 15 minutes
before the scheduled examination time.

Laboratory:
Students must follow all laboratory safety instructions.
Laboratory equipment must be handled carefully and any damage
must immediately be reported to the laboratory instructor.

Discipline:
Students are expected to maintain proper discipline within
the college campus. Any behavior that violates college rules
may result in disciplinary action.
